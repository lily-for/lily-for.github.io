PROJECT / 01

NeuroSense

Adaptive sensory feedback glove · ASME Make-a-Thon

A 48-hour team project exploring how temperature sensing can give wearable users clear alerts through light and sound. Our five-person team earned 3rd place at the 2026 ASME Make-a-Thon.

My work focused on translating skin injury research into meaningful temperature-alert thresholds, specifying LED and buzzer behaviors, and testing the Teensy-based thermistor response under hot and cold conditions.

PROJECT / 02

FPV Drone

Flight controller integration · ASME IAM3D Competition

Built and integrated the electrical systems for an FPV drone, including flight-controller and electronic speed-controller connections, and helped troubleshoot the configuration in Betaflight.

Installed dual cameras and a video switcher for selectable pilot views, while working around space constraints inside a 3D-printed frame.

PROJECT / 03

Star Sniper

Smart telescope · Internet of Things course

Developed the tripod and leveling subsystem for a smart telescope prototype designed to orient toward user-selected stars.

Worked with three linear actuators for coarse leveling and three servos intended for fine adjustment of the telescope platform. The project brought together mechanical positioning, microcontrollers, and physical system integration.

PROJECT / 04

Autonomous Garbage Truck

ASME Student Design Competition

Contributed to a student-designed vehicle intended to navigate a model city while collecting and sorting waste. Our team earned 2nd place in the 2025–2026 ASME Student Design Competition.

I worked with sensors and microcontrollers for waste identification and remote control, and participated in team testing and iteration.
